# EARTHMIND: Environmental Digital Twin & Future Simulation Platform
## Project Specifications & Core Blueprint

### 1. Vision & Identity
- **Tagline**: "Explore Earth's Past. Understand Its Present. Simulate Its Future."
- **Exhibition**: Young Scientist '26 Science Exhibition
- **Goal**: Premium, futuristic environmental intelligence SaaS platform combining satellite forensics, real-time indicator tracking, dynamic multi-variable "WHAT-IF?" simulations, scenario comparison, transparent AI decision support, and presentation-ready reporting.

### 2. Design System
- **Style**: Liquid Glass, Soft 3D Depth, Translucent Frosted Glass, Ambient Aurora Glow.
- **Palette**:
  - Deep Ocean (`#071A2B`)

### 3. Core Modules
1. **Interactive 3D Earth Digital Twin**:
   - WebGL Three.js Sphere with realistic atmosphere halo shader, land/ocean textures, night lights, floating data points, environmental hotspot coordinates.
   - Interactive layer overlays: Temperature, Air Quality, Green Cover, Water Bodies, Urbanization, Waste Risk, Flood Risk, Environmental Health.
2. **Global Command Center (Ctrl+K)**:
   - Natural language queries, quick navigation, scenario presets, action execution.
3. **Earth Memory (Temporal Forensics)**:
   - 2010 - 2026 temporal scrubber.
   - Before/After split comparison & animated transition.
   - Change indicators (Vegetation loss, Built-up area expansion, Surface temperature variance, Water surface change).
4. **Environmental Forensics**:
   - Multi-factor anomaly correlation engine with confidence weighting.
   - Clearly disclaims correlation vs certainty ("Model-associated factor", "Detected correlation").
5. **WHAT-IF Simulation Engine (Hero Feature)**:
   - 7 Reactive environmental policy/climate levers:
     - Tree Cover (-20% to +50%)
     - Rainfall (-30% to +60%)
     - Urbanization (-20% to +50%)
     - Waste Generation (-30% to +70%)
     - Water Availability (-50% to +50%)
     - Traffic Density (-30% to +60%)
     - Energy Efficiency (-30% to +50%)
   - Dynamic non-linear simulation formulas recalculating Heat Island Risk, Flood Vulnerability, Air Pollution Index, Water Stress, and Composite Environmental Health Score.
6. **Scenario Laboratory & Multi-Scenario Comparison**:
   - Side-by-side matrices: Baseline, Green City 2030, High Urban Growth, Climate Stress, and Custom Saved Scenarios.
   - Radar charts, delta badges (Improvement / Degradation / Unchanged), composite sustainability rankings.
7. **AI Decision Engine & Context-Aware Assistant (EARTHMIND AI)**:
   - Transparent, evidence-based recommendations (Priority 1, 2, 3) with limitation disclosures.
   - Context-aware conversational assistant aware of currently selected hotspot, active layer, timeline year, and simulation metrics.
8. **Guided Demo Mode**:
   - 2-3 minute automated exhibition showcase walking judges through the entire end-to-end loop.
9. **Science Exhibition Mode**:
   - Clean, academic slide-over mode: Problem -> Method -> Data -> AI Model -> Simulation -> Result -> Impact -> Conclusion.
10. **Executive Environmental Intelligence Report**:
    - PDF/Print-ready multi-page report generator with full findings, radar charts, delta breakdown, and metadata stamp.
